Rogue Star
==========

Whales' game of space trading and exploration (2013 to 2014), "think Star
Control 2, but more roguelikelike", finished for Asciiverse. You owe the
bank 8,000 credits for the freighter Vindicator: trade fourteen goods
across a dozen worlds, deal with pirates on the lawless lanes, land survey
teams on uncharted worlds, and either clear the debt by the end of the
year 3004 or follow three Precursor chart fragments to the Vault.

This build is the Asciiverse edition of github.com/Whales/rogue-star, from
github.com/theasciiverse/rogue-star; PATCHES.md there lists every change.

Running it
----------

Start ./roguestar in a terminal of at least 80x24. It reads its screens
from cuss/ beside the program. Your voyage and your scores are saved in
$XDG_STATE_HOME/rogue-star (usually ~/.local/state/rogue-star), or in
~/Library/Application Support/rogue-star on macOS.

On the main screen: S ship, M star map (travel), T trade, D dock,
V survey, H hire crew, ? help, Q save and quit.
